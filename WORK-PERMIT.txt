WORK PERMIT — implementation and rollout notes

Entry: /permit (existing EA SHE Tools application)
Branch: codex/work-permit

IMPLEMENTED
- Dedicated contractor accounts and cookie; existing staff use Tools login.
- Contractor starts_at/expires_at and active status checked on every API request.
- Contractor sees only own permits in assigned company. No access to PPE/Chemical from permit cookie.
- Admin creates contractor accounts, suspends them and assigns existing internal accounts to signer roles.
- Company-scoped, versioned template editor; AMITA 9-template seed import starts inactive.
- General and specialist forms in one work package; server-owned template snapshot.
- Draft/edit, submit, ordered approval, site-owner start, suspend/resume, inspection, ordered closure, cancellation.
- Extension/revision creates a separate request linked to the original and requires new signatures.
- Signature drawing or image upload, explicit confirmation, actor/time/document SHA-256.
- Returned requests retain the prior signed snapshot in the audit event and mark old signatures superseded.
- Optimistic concurrency on every permit update; append-only audit and immutable final documents.
- Small PDF/PNG/JPG attachments, authenticated source PDF viewing, printable digital document and authenticated QR verification.
- Responsive Thai UI, company selector, status filtering and search over latest 100 records.

DOCUMENT REVIEW REQUIRED
- Imported digital checklists are summarized editable transcriptions, not exact facsimiles.
- AHS-005 supplied PDF contains Rev.D on page 1 and Rev.B on page 2.
- AHS-006 CO/H2S labels and units in the original table require owner review.
- No numeric gas limits are interpreted or auto-approved by this application.
- AMITA source PDFs and SHA-256 source manifest are under data/permit-templates.
- Original PDFs are served behind permit authorization; not placed in public/.

CURRENT LIMITS / NEXT IMPLEMENTATION
- Original-template PDF export overlays recorded job details and workflow signatures on all supplied AMITA PDF pages, preserving original revision/layout. The original-template action is limited to matching AMT source revisions; other templates retain digital summary printing.
- Digital checklists are broader than some paper rows. Individual paper checkboxes, gas measurement cells, and roles without an exact recorded equivalent remain blank; complete answers, long values, signer identities/hashes and events are printed in labelled annexes. No safety results are inferred.
- On-site checks and gas readings use text entries, not structured hourly measurement tables.
- Overtime/extension is a new linked request, not a separate same-document overtime approval workflow.
- Attachments are limited to 8 files, 350 KB each, stored in private permit JSON. Move to private object storage before increasing size.
- Dashboard shows latest 100 requests. Server-side history pagination and aggregate reporting are pending.
- No automated reminder delivery or area/time conflict detection yet.
- Contractors do not share each other's permits even when they have the same contractor company name.
- Production migration, deployment, and company acceptance of templates are not part of local test evidence.

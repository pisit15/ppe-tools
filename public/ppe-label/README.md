# PPE label artwork

Six SVG files are local copies of the following Wikimedia Commons drawings of ISO 7010 signs. Retrieved 2026-10-10. The file-description pages/API identify each drawing as Public domain. Geometry and source colours are retained; no external network requests are needed at runtime.

boots.svg (side-view high boot), dust-mask.svg (cup-shaped mask) and scba.svg are original EA SHE Tools illustrations. Their blue/white palette matches the imported drawings. They are not ISO sign originals and no ISO certification is claimed for them or for the selector as a whole. The generic ISO mask M016 was not used for the dust-filter category because its artwork depicts a medical-style mask.

The sign images indicate equipment categories. They do not establish chemical compatibility, cartridge type, breakthrough time or other product specifications. The selector and PDF captions identify the intended selection; the user chooses equipment appropriate to the product SDS. Stable equipment codes preserve existing saved label selections.

All SVGs are self-contained vector geometry, without scripts, external references, raster images or fonts.
